
**Tänane WOTD**
Seire - Nimisõna/Noun
*seire, seire, seiret; seired, seirete, seireid*

**Tähendused/Meanings:**
🇪🇪 **1.** pidev ühiskonna, keskkonna, tervise vm seisundi jälgimine,pidev seisundi jälgimine
🇬🇧 **1.** continuous monitoring of society, environment, health, etc. condition, continuous monitoring of condition

**Näited/Examples:**
● Nahkhiirte seires uusi liike sel aastal ei leitud. ||No new species were found in bat monitoring this year.||
● Maakoore seismiline seire. ||Seismic monitoring of the Earth's crust.||
● Piirivalvurid kasutavad seireks lasertehnoloogiat. ||Border guards use laser technology for monitoring.||
● Kolme aasta jooksul kogutud seire tulemused on väga ootuspärased. ||The monitoring results collected over the past three years are very much as expected.||
● Läbiviidud seirete käigus leiti mürkaineid. ||Toxic substances were detected during the monitoring activities.||
