
**Tänane WOTD**
Saladus - Nimisõna/Noun
*saladus, saladuse, saladust; saladused, saladuste, saladusi*

**Tähendused/Meanings:**
🇪🇪 **1.** (seni) teadmata, seletamatu asjaolu,salajas, varjatuna peetav või olev teadmine,miski, mida teavad ainult vähesed inimesed ja mida ei taheta teistele öelda
🇪🇪 **2.** millegi varjamine, pidamine salajasena,sellisena säilitamise kohustus
🇪🇪 **3.** millegi (varjatud) põhjus
🇪🇪 **4.** millekski vajalik sügavam (seni omandamata) teadmine, oskus, võte vms
🇬🇧 **1.** (hitherto) unknown, unexplained fact, secret, hidden or existing knowledge,something that only a few people know and do not want to say to others
🇬🇧 **2.** hiding something, keeping it secret, obligation to keep it that way
🇬🇧 **3.** the reason for something (hidden)
🇬🇧 **4.** deeper (not yet acquired) knowledge, skill, technique, etc. needed for something

**Näited/Examples:**
● See saladus paljastus päris kiiresti. ||That secret was revealed pretty quickly.||
● Õppustel toimunu on riiklik saladus. ||What happened during the exercises is a national secret.||
● Mehe hingel on sünge saladus. ||A man's soul has a dark secret.||
● Maailmas on veel palju lahendamata saladusi. ||There are still many unresolved mysteries in the world.||
● Ema pikaealisuse saladuseks peab tütar geene ja lihtsat maaelu. ||The secret of a mother's longevity is her daughter's genes and simple rural life.||
● Mis on õnneliku abielu saladus? ||What is the secret to a happy marriage?||
