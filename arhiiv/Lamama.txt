
**Tänane WOTD**
Lamama - Tegusõna/Verb
*lamama, lamada; lamab, lamas; lamanud, lamatud*

**Tähendused/Meanings:**
🇪🇪 **1.** (inimese või looma kohta:) pikali olema,inimeste ja loomade kohta: sellises asendis olema, kus kogu keha toetub mingile pinnale, pikali olema
🇪🇪 **2.** (eseme vm kohta, tavalisem lebama:) rõhtsalt asetsema
🇬🇧 **1.** (about a person or an animal) to be lying down, about people and animals: to be in such a position where the whole body rests on some surface, to be lying down
🇬🇧 **2.** (of an object etc., more common to lie down) to lie flat

**Näited/Examples:**
● Enam muud ei tahagi kui kuumal rannaliival lamada. ||All you want is to lie on the hot beach sand.||
● Koer lamab põõsa all varjus. ||The dog lies in the shade under a bush.||
● Haigel kästi lamada. ||The patient was told to lie down.||
● Naine lamab diivanil. ||The woman is lying on the couch.||
● Koer lamab põrandal. ||The dog is lying on the floor.||
● Haige lamas voodis. ||The patient was lying in bed.||
● Uppunud laev lamab umbes 50 meetri sügavusel. ||The sunken ship lies at a depth of about 50 meters.||
