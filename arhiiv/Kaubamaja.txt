
**Tänane WOTD**
Kaubamaja - Nimisõna/Noun
*kaubamaja, kaubamaja, kaubamaja; kaubamajad, kaubamajade, kaubamaju/kaubamajasid*

**Tähendused/Meanings:**
🇪🇪 **1.** suur paljude osakondadega ja laia valikuga kauplus (hrl omaette hoones),mitmest osakonnast koosnev suur kauplus, kus on lai valik tooteid
🇬🇧 **1.** a large store with many departments and a wide range of products (in a separate building), a large store consisting of several departments with a wide range of products

**Näited/Examples:**
● Kammisime õiget värvi kotti otsides kõik kaubamajad läbi. ||We combed every department store looking for the right color bag.||
● Ostsin kaubamajast uue ülikonna. ||I bought a new suit at the department store.||
● Hotellist üle tee on suur kaubamaja. ||Across the street from the hotel is a large department store.||
● Ostsin kaubamajast kõige populaarsema deodorandi. ||I bought the most popular deodorant at the department store.||
● Õhtul läksin linna äärde asuvasse kaubamajja uudistama. ||In the evening, I went to the department store on the outskirts of town to check it out.||
