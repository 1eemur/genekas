Peavalu - Nimisõna/Noun
*peavalu, peavalu, peavalu; peavalud, peavalude, peavalusid*

**Tähendused/Meanings:**
🇪🇪 **1.** valu peas
🇪🇪 **2.** mure, meelehärm
🇬🇧 **1.** pain in the head
🇬🇧 **2.** worry, frustration

**Näited/Examples:**
● Laps kurdab peavalu. ||The child complains of a headache.||
● Mul oli eile kohutav peavalu. ||I had a terrible headache yesterday.||
● Kuidas ravite peavalu ja unetust? ||How do you treat headaches and insomnia?||
● Hommikul oli peavalu, õhtul juba palavik. ||I had a headache in the morning, already a fever in the evening.||
● Tundsin, et mul on jube peavalu ja väga paha olla. ||I felt like I had a terrible headache and was feeling really sick.||
