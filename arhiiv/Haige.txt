
**Tänane WOTD**
Haige - Omadussõna/Adjective
*haige, haige, haiget; haiged, haigete, haigeid*

**Tähendused/Meanings:**
🇪🇪 **1.** mingit haigust põdev või halvas tervislikus seisundis olev, mitteterve,selline, kelle tervis pole korras, kellel on mingi haigus
🇪🇪 **2.** valu tegev, valutav
🇪🇪 **3.** (hingelise seisundi kohta:) murelik, kurb, nukker
🇪🇪 **4.** inimene, kes põeb mingit haigust, mitteterve inimene,inimene, kellel on mingi haigus
🇪🇪 **5.** midagi kirglikult harrastav
🇪🇪 **6.** midagi kirglikult harrastav inimene
🇪🇪 **7.** ebanormaalne, tobe
🇬🇧 **1.** suffering from any disease or in poor health, unhealthy, unhealthy, with any disease
🇬🇧 **2.** painful
🇬🇧 **3.** (about mental state) anxious, sad
🇬🇧 **4.** a person suffering from a disease, an unhealthy person, a person with a disease
🇬🇧 **5.** something passionately engaging
🇬🇧 **6.** a person who is passionate about something
🇬🇧 **7.** abnormal, silly

**Näited/Examples:**
● Vanaisa on juba vana ja haige. ||Grandpa is already old and sick.||
● Kopsud on haiged. ||Lungs are sick.||
● Ta on väga haige. ||He's very sick.||
● Arst kutsuti haige juurde. ||The doctor was called in to see the patient.||
● Haige paranes kiiresti. ||The patient recovered quickly.||
● Täiesti haige värk. ||Totally sick stuff.||
