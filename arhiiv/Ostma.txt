Ostma - Tegusõna/Verb
*ostma, osta; ostab, ostis; ostnud, ostetud*

**Tähendused/Meanings:**
🇪🇪 **1.** raha eest midagi vastu saama,raha eest midagi saama
🇪🇪 **2.** kaardimängus reeglite järgi oma kaartidele pakist lisa saama
🇬🇧 **1.** to buy, to get something for money, to get something for money
🇬🇧 **2.** in the card game, according to the rules, to get more cards from your pack

**Näited/Examples:**
● Tõnis ostis uue auto. ||Tõnis bought a new car.||
● Poiss ostis endale uue arvuti. ||The boy bought himself a new computer.||
● Ostsin sõbralt telefoni. ||I bought a phone from a friend.||
● Ostsin selle raamatu kümne euro eest. ||I bought this book for ten euros.||
● Palun osta poest pakk jogurtit! ||Please buy a pack of yogurt from the store!||
