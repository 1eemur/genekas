
**Tänane WOTD**
Investeerima - Tegusõna/Verb
*investeerima, investeerida; investeerib, investeeris; investeerinud, investeeritud*

**Tähendused/Meanings:**
🇪🇪 **1.** oma raha paigutama väärtpaberitesse, kinnisvarasse vm viisil, et sellest tulu teenida,ettevõttele oma raha andma või näiteks aktsiaid ostma, et hiljem sellest tulu saada,raha paigutama
🇬🇧 **1.** to invest your money in securities, real estate, etc., in order to earn income from it, to give your money to a company or, for example, to buy shares in order to receive income from it later, to invest money

**Näited/Examples:**
● Esimene võimalus on investeerida raha tähtajalisse hoiusesse. ||The first option is to invest the money in a term deposit.||
● Juba mitmendat aastat investeeritakse miljoneid teedeehitusse. ||Millions have been invested in road construction for several years.||
● Kui palju raha sa oled investeerinud? ||How much money have you invested?||
● Ta investeeris oma firmasse ligi 70 000 eurot. ||He invested nearly 70,000 euros in his company.||
● Otsustasin investeerida kinnisvarasse. ||I decided to invest in real estate.||
