
**Tänane WOTD**
Kurg - Nimisõna/Noun
*kurg, kure, kurge; kured, kurgede, kurgi/kurgesid*

**Tähendused/Meanings:**
🇪🇪 **1.** suur, pika noka ja kaela ning kõrgete jalgadega lind, hrl toonekure kohta,suur hall lind, kellel on pikk kael, pikk pruun nokk ja pikad mustad jalad,lind
🇪🇪 **2.** (rahvauskumuses:) toonekurg lastetoojana,suur valge lind, kelle tiibade otsad on mustad, aga jalad ja nokk on punased
🇬🇧 **1.** crane, large bird with long beak and neck and high legs, large grey bird with long neck, long brown beak and long black legs
🇬🇧 **2.** (Folk belief) stork as a bringer of children, a large white bird whose wing tips are black but whose feet and beak are red

**Näited/Examples:**
● Kurg seisab vahel ka ühe jala peal. ||The crane sometimes stands on one leg.||
● Sügisesed kurgede kolmnurgad taeva all. ||Autumn crane triangles under the sky.||
● Kured lendavad talveks soojale maale. ||The cranes fly to warm land for the winter.||
● Seal peres oodatakse pikisilmi kurge. ||The storks are eagerly awaited in that family.||
● Posti otsas oli suur kurgede pesa. ||There was a large crane nest at the end of the post.||
