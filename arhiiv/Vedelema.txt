
**Tänane WOTD**
Vedelema - Tegusõna/Verb
*vedelema, vedeleda; vedeleb, vedeles; vedelenud, vedeletud*

**Tähendused/Meanings:**
🇪🇪 **1.** (elusolendi kohta:) (laisana, roidununa, mõnuledes) lamama või lösutama,lamama ja laisk olema
🇪🇪 **2.** olesklema,laisklema
🇪🇪 **3.** (asjade kohta:) korratult, lohakil, mahajäetuna või äravisatuna lebama, mitte omal kohal olema,kui mingi asi vedeleb, siis see on nt põrandal või mujal, kus see olema ei peaks
🇬🇧 **1.** (about a living being) (lazy, lazy, enjoying) to lie down or lounging, to lie down and be lazy
🇬🇧 **2.** to linger, to laze around
🇬🇧 **3.** (about things) to lie disorderly, sloppy, abandoned or thrown away, not to be in its place, if something is floating, it is e.g. on the floor or somewhere else where it shouldn't be

**Näited/Examples:**
● Isa vedeles diivanil. ||Dad was lounging on the couch.||
● Goa on koht, kus võib lihtsalt vedelda ja mitte midagi teha. ||Goa is a place where you can just hang around and do nothing.||
● Külmkapis vedeles kaks kotletti. ||There were two cutlets laying around in the fridge.||
● Mehe ametirelv vedeles ta kõrval pingil. ||The man's service weapon was lying on the bench next to him.||
● Tänaval vedeleb laip! ||A corpse is laying in the street!||
● Mänguasjad vedelesid elutoa põrandal. ||Toys were laying around on the living room floor.||
