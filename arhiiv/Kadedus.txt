Kadedus - Nimisõna/Noun
*kadedus, kadeduse, kadedust; kadedused, kadeduste, kadedusi*

**Tähendused/Meanings:**
🇪🇪 **1.** meelehärm, kibestumine vm (negatiivse varjundiga) tunne teiste saavutuste, edu, heaolu vms puhul, selle kõige endale ihkamine
🇬🇧 **1.** envy, a feeling of resentment, bitterness, or the like (with negative connotations) toward others’ achievements, success, well-being, etc., wishing all of that for oneself
