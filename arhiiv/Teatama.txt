
**Tänane WOTD**
Teatama - Tegusõna/Verb
*teatama, teatada; teatab, teatas; teatanud, teatatud*

**Tähendused/Meanings:**
🇪🇪 **1.** teadet, uudist kellelegi edasi andma, millestki teada andma, midagi teatavaks tegema,infot, teadet (edasi) andma,midagi teatavaks tegema
🇬🇧 **1.** to send a message, news to someone, to report something, to make something known, to provide information, to (pass on) a message, to make something known

**Näited/Examples:**
● „Lendame otsekursil,” teatas lendur. ||“We are flying on a direct course,” said the pilot.||
● Kui midagi leiad, teata kohe mulle. ||If you find anything, let me know right away.||
● Kallaletungist teatati politseisse. ||The assault was reported to the police.||
● Õde helistas ja teatas kurva uudise. ||The nurse called with the sad news.||
● Teatasin perearstile oma uue aadressi. ||I informed my family doctor of my new address.||
● Mees teatas õnnetusest politseisse. ||The man reported the accident to the police.||
● Ajaleht teatab, et homme tuleb ilus ilm. ||The newspaper reports that the weather will be beautiful tomorrow.||
