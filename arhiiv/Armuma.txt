
**Tänane WOTD**
Armuma - Tegusõna/Verb
*armuma, armuda; armub, armus; armunud, armutud*

**Tähendused/Meanings:**
🇪🇪 **1.** kellessegi või millessegi (äkki, erutust tundes) kiinduma, kedagi või midagi armastama hakkama,kedagi armastama hakkama
🇬🇧 **1.** to fall in love, fall for, to become attached to someone or something (suddenly, feeling excited), to love someone or something, to love someone

**Näited/Examples:**
● Ma olen armunud. ||I'm in love.||
● Poiss armus tüdrukusse esimesel kohtumisel. ||The boy fell in love with the girl at the first meeting.||
● Tegu on imeilusa linnaga – armusin sellesse kohe. ||It's a beautiful city - I fell in love with it immediately.||
● Noored armusid , seejärel abiellusid ja neil sündis poeg. ||The young couple fell in love, then got married, and had a son.||
● Eestisse tulnud soome mees armus oma keeleõpetajasse. ||A Finnish man who came to Estonia fell in love with his language teacher.||
