
**Tänane WOTD**
Peaminister - Nimisõna/Noun
*peaminister, peaministri, peaministrit; peaministrid, peaministrite, peaministreid*

**Tähendused/Meanings:**
🇪🇪 **1.** valitsuse (ministrite kabineti) juht, kelle hrl nimetab ametisse riigipea,valitsuse juht. Eestis nimetab peaministri ametisse president
🇬🇧 **1.** prime minister, head of Government (Cabinet of Ministers) appointed by Head of State, Head of Government. In Estonia, the Prime Minister is appointed by the President

**Näited/Examples:**
● Kes saab uueks peaministriks? ||Who will be the new Prime Minister?||
● Peaminister lükkas süüdistused vihaselt tagasi. ||The prime minister vehemently denied the allegations.||
● Uus peaminister teatas, et valitsusele on esimesed ülesanded juba jaotatud. ||The new prime minister announced that the government’s first tasks have already been assigned.||
● Peaminister teatas tagasiastumisest. ||The prime minister announced his resignation.||
● President määras peaministriks endise välisministri. ||The President appointed the former foreign minister as Prime Minister.||
