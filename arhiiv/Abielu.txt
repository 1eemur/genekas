
**Tänane WOTD**
Abielu - Nimisõna/Noun
*abielu, abielu, abielu; abielud, abielude, abielusid*

**Tähendused/Meanings:**
🇪🇪 **1.** kahe inimese ametlikult registreeritud perekondlik liit ning kooselu,mehe ja naise seaduslik liit
🇬🇧 **1.** marriage, officially registered family union of two people and legal union of cohabitation

**Näited/Examples:**
● Abielu sõlmiti Inglismaal, mis tõi endaga kaasa hulga paberlikke toiminguid. ||The marriage took place in England, which brought with it a lot of paperwork.||
● Milles peitub pika ja õnneliku abielu saladus? ||What is the secret to a long and happy marriage?||
● USAs arutab samasooliste abielude legaliseerimist Massachusettsi ülemkohus. ||In the United States, the Supreme Court of Massachusetts is considering legalizing same-sex marriage.||
● Pooled inimesed on elu jooksul kogenud abielu või kooselu purunemist. ||Half the people in their lives have experienced a breakdown in marriage or cohabitation.||
● Neil oli pikk ja õnnelik abielu. ||They had a long and happy marriage.||
