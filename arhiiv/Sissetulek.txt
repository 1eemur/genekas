Sissetulek - Nimisõna/Noun
*sissetulek, sissetuleku, sissetulekut; sissetulekud, sissetulekute, sissetulekuid*

**Tähendused/Meanings:**
🇪🇪 **1.** teatud aja jooksul laekunud rahasumma, rahaline tulu,saadud hüviste hulk,(teatud aja jooksul) teenitud raha, tulu
🇪🇪 **2.** kuhugi, mingile alale tulemine
🇬🇧 **1.** the amount of money received during a certain period of time, financial income, the amount of benefits received, (during a certain period of time) money earned, income
🇬🇧 **2.** coming somewhere, to some area

**Näited/Examples:**
● Leibkondade sissetulekud ja väljaminekud. ||Household income and expenditure.||
● Kinnisvarasse investeerimine annab suhteliselt kindlat ja püsivat sissetulekut. ||Investing in real estate provides a relatively stable and stable income.||
● Kui suur on eestlaste keskmine sissetulek? ||What is the average income of Estonians?||
● Perel on kindel sissetulek. ||The family has a fixed income.||
● Vanahärra rääkis oma mälestusi Vene vägede sissetulekust 1940. aastal. ||The old man told his memories of the arrival of Russian troops in 1940.||
