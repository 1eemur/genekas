
**Tänane WOTD**
Remont - Nimisõna/Noun
*remont, remondi, remonti; remondid, remontide, remonte/remontisid*

**Tähendused/Meanings:**
🇪🇪 **1.** hoonete, seadmete, teede vms parandamine ja uuendamine,hoonete, sõidukite kohta: parandamine, korda tegemine
🇪🇪 **2.** (loomakasvatuses:) karja uuendamine ja täiendamine
🇬🇧 **1.** repair and refurbishment of buildings, equipment, roads, etc., about buildings, vehicles: repairs, repairs
🇬🇧 **2.** (in animal husbandry) renewal and replenishment of livestock

**Näited/Examples:**
● Esimesel korrusel on tehtud remont, teisel mitte. ||The first floor has been repaired, the second floor has not.||
● Teeremont. ||Road repair.||
● Hoone remont lõpeb sügisel. ||The renovation of the building will be completed in the autumn.||
● Viisin auto remonti. ||I took the car in for repairs.||
● Karja remont praegu veel kestab, varsti tulevad peale ka noored loomad. ||The herd is still being renewed; young animals will be added soon.||
